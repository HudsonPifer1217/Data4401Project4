# Features

 - `people_main_subject`: `content` A human being is the main subject of the image.
    - Edge Cases: 
         - A person in the background does not count.
         - Multiple people as the main subject count.
 - `street_or_road`: `content` A street, road, or sidewalk is visible.
 - `sunset_sunrie`: `content` Sun near the horizon, or an orange/pink sky
 - `leading_lines`: `composition` A street, path, or row of buildings pulls the eye toward a vanishing point.
 - `close_up`: `composition` The main subject fills more than half the frame
